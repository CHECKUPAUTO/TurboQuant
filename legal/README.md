# legal/ — Commercial license draft (TurboQuant)

## Status

`COMMERCIAL_LICENSE_DRAFT.md` is a **draft template**, prepared **without
attorney involvement**. It is not legal advice, it is **not in force**, and it
**creates no rights or obligations** until it has been reviewed and adapted by
qualified counsel and executed by both parties together with an Order Form.
Do not sign or rely on it in its current form.

## How it fits the dual-license model

- [`LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md) defines the model: PolyForm Noncommercial
  1.0.0 (free, [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md)) for any noncommercial use, plus
  a paid commercial license offered **exclusively for use of TurboQuant as a
  CCOS module** (alongside SLHAv2, e.g. the TQ3 tile codec and the CCOS
  Soft-Paging correction rung).
- This draft is the template for that commercial arm (LICENSING.md section 2,
  today materialized only by the contact email contact@checkupauto.fr). It
  does not modify, replace or condition the PolyForm Noncommercial License,
  which remains an independent public license.
- Prior versions published under MIT OR Apache-2.0 keep their original terms
  (LICENSING.md section 3) and are outside the draft's scope.
- Contributions accepted under the project CLA (LICENSING.md section 5) flow
  into both arms: the draft's Section 5.4 mirrors this.
- The companion CCOS tile kernel SLHAv2 has the parallel draft in its own
  repository (`SLHAv2/legal/COMMERCIAL_LICENSE_DRAFT.md`); the two share the
  same agreement body.

## Execution checklist (before any use)

1. Resolve every [BRACKETED] business decision: licensor capacity/entity and
   address, deployment option (A internal / B distribution), sublicensing for
   embedded products, fees and pricing model, payment/late-interest terms,
   warranty option, liability cap, term/renewal, audit and DPA specifics.
2. Have the draft **reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel** in both
   target frameworks (EU/France and US), including the warranty/liability
   carve-outs, tax clauses and export classification.
3. Define the **Order Form** (Exhibit A outline) — it is the commercial
   vehicle that gives the agreement effect and records the elected options,
   including the governing-law choice (France/Paris vs Delaware; one per
   Order Form).
4. Execute: both parties sign the final agreement and Order Form (electronic
   signature per eIDAS / ESIGN acceptable). Only then does anything bind.
